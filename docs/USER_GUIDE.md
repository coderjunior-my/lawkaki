# Law Kaki — User Guide

*For the lawyers actually using the app. Written for how it works today, not how it's someday meant to.*

---

## What is Law Kaki?

Your best legal kaki on the ground. Post a signing you can't make, and a nearby colleague picks it up. You see each other's contact once confirmed, get reminders before the appointment, and rate each other after.

Most lawyers use it as **both** — posting when they're double-booked, picking up when they have a gap.

---

## 1. Getting started

1. Open the Law Kaki link (shared via WhatsApp, or go straight to the site).
2. If you're logged out, you'll land on the intro page — tap **Get started**.
3. Enter your mobile number (Malaysian, e.g. `012-3456789`). Tap **Send verification code**.
4. Check WhatsApp for a 6-digit code. It auto-verifies as soon as you type the last digit — no need to tap anything.
   - *Didn't get it?* Wait for the 30-second countdown to finish, then **Resend code**.
5. **First time only** — set up your profile:
   - Full name
   - Your law firm (search from the list — every state is covered, not just KL/Selangor)
   - Law firm email (used to verify you're a practising lawyer — nothing is ever sent to it)
   - What you'll use Law Kaki for: **Post jobs**, **Pick jobs**, or **Both**
6. Tap **Create account**. You're in.

Your session stays signed in for 30 days.

---

## 2. Your dashboard

The map/list view is your home screen. Job pins are colour-coded:
- **Amber pin** — open, still needs a picker
- **Black pin** — already taken

Use the filter bar to narrow by date, document type, or minimum fee. Tap a pin or list item to see the job's full details.

---

## 3. Posting a job

1. Tap **Post a job**.
2. Fill in: document type, venue, address, area (optional but helps pickers filter), date & time, indicative commission (RM), and any notes the picker should know.
3. Tap **Post job**.

That's it — every eligible lawyer on Law Kaki is notified on WhatsApp that a new job is open near them, and it appears on their map as an amber pin.

**What happens next:**
- Interested pickers show up on your job as "interest expressed" — tap to view their profile (rating breakdown, firm, job count) before deciding.
- Tap **Confirm** on the picker you want. You'll both get a WhatsApp with each other's contact.
- Everyone else who expressed interest is automatically declined — no action needed from you.
- If your plans change, you can **cancel** the job (only while it's still open/unconfirmed).
- Once the appointment's done, come back and tap **Mark complete**, then rate your picker.

> **If nobody confirms within a few days:** we'll nudge you on WhatsApp on day 3 and day 7 to remind you interested pickers are waiting. If you still haven't responded by day 9, that interest quietly expires — the job itself stays open.

---

## 4. Picking up a job

1. Browse the map/list for open (amber) jobs.
2. Tap a job you can take, review the details, and tap **I'm interested**.
3. The poster is notified on WhatsApp and in-app. Now you wait for them to confirm you.
4. If confirmed: you'll get a WhatsApp with the poster's contact, and the job moves to **My tasks → Jobs to work on**.
5. You'll get automatic reminders **2 hours** and **30 minutes** before the appointment.
6. On the day: open the job and mark each stage as you go — **signing done**, then **runner collected docs** (if documents need to physically move). The poster is notified at each step and gives the final confirmation once they've received everything.

---

## 5. My tasks

Tap the tasks icon in the top bar for a three-tab view of everything needing your attention:

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Needs your confirmation** | Jobs you posted that have pickers waiting for you to confirm one |
| **Jobs to work on** | Jobs you've picked and are confirmed for |
| **Give review** | Completed jobs you haven't rated yet |

This is the fastest way to see what's actually pending on you, without scrolling the whole map.

---

## 6. Notifications

Tap the **bell icon** for your notification inbox.

- An **amber dot** appears on the bell only when you have unread notifications — no dot means you're caught up.
- If your account is set to **Both**, notifications split into **As picker** / **As poster** tabs, so you can tell which hat an alert belongs to. If you're only one role, you'll just see one list.
- Tap any notification to mark it read.
- **Mark all as read** clears everything currently in view (i.e. just the tab you're looking at).
- Everything that reaches you on WhatsApp is also logged here — so even if a WhatsApp message doesn't arrive, it's not lost.

---

## 7. Ratings

After a job is marked complete, both sides rate each other on three things, 1–5 stars each:

- **Punctuality** — did they arrive on time?
- **Professionalism** — did they represent the firm well?
- **Completeness** — were all documents handled correctly?

You can add an optional comment, up to 1,000 words.

**A note on new accounts:** your rating won't show publicly until you've completed **3 jobs** — this protects new lawyers from being judged on too little data. Before that, other users see "Fewer than 3 completed jobs."

---

## 8. Settings — what works today

Settings has four tabs: Profile, Job history, Upcoming, Reviews.

**Heads up:** as of this version, the **Settings panel runs on sample data and doesn't save to your real account.** Editing your name, email, or firm here won't persist — and the History/Upcoming/Reviews tabs inside Settings show placeholder jobs, not your actual ones.

**For anything that matters, use these instead** — they're the real, live version of the same information:
- Your actual job history and in-progress jobs → the main dashboard and **My tasks**
- Giving or checking reviews → **My tasks → Give review**

If you spot this note still here in a later version, it means Settings hasn't been wired up to live data yet — worth flagging to whoever's maintaining the app.

## 9. FAQ

**I didn't get my OTP.** Make sure WhatsApp is open on the number you entered. Wait for the resend countdown, then try again. If it keeps failing, the team's WhatsApp sending may be temporarily down — try again shortly.

**Why can't I see a picker's rating?** They haven't completed 3 jobs yet (see §7).

**Can lawyers from other firms see and pick up my job?** Yes — Law Kaki currently covers every firm nationwide, not just your own. Anyone registered can see and pick up any open job, and you can do the same with theirs.

**How do I get paid?** Commission is indicative and settled directly between you and the poster (or via your firm's internal process) — there's no in-app payment yet.

**How do I change my role (Poster/Picker/Both)?** Not available yet in the live app — check with whoever manages your account.

**I moved cities — can I still pick up jobs there?** Yes. Coverage is nationwide, so you can pick up jobs wherever you are, not just where you're normally based.
