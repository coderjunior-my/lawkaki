# Law Kaki — What It Does, For Your Firm

*A plain explanation for a managing partner or ops manager deciding whether to bring Law Kaki into the firm.*

---

## The problem you already know

A conveyancing lawyer's afternoon: Ara Damansara at 2pm, Mont Kiara at 4pm, Bangsar at 6pm. Three signings, three different clients, and an entire day lost to KL traffic for what's often 20 minutes of actual legal work each time.

Delegating to a colleague nearby already happens — informally, over a WhatsApp message to whoever's free. It works, but it's ad hoc: no visibility into who's actually available, no record of who covered what, no accountability if it goes wrong.

Law Kaki turns that informal habit into a structured, trackable system.

---

## How it works

1. **Post.** A lawyer who can't make a signing posts it — venue, time, document type, indicative fee.
2. **Notify.** Every eligible lawyer on the platform gets a WhatsApp alert and sees it on their map.
3. **Confirm.** Interested lawyers put their hand up; the poster picks one and confirms. Both sides get each other's contact.
4. **Remind & complete.** Automatic reminders fire 2 hours and 30 minutes before the appointment. Afterward, the poster marks it complete and rates the lawyer who covered it.

No app download — it runs in the browser, entered through a WhatsApp link.

---

## What's already live

- **Nationwide, not just KL/Selangor.** Coverage spans all 13 states and 3 federal territories.
- **Cross-firm.** As it stands today, Law Kaki is an open marketplace — a lawyer at your firm can pick up a job posted by a lawyer at a different firm, and vice versa. It is **not** currently scoped to keep jobs inside a single firm. If your firm wants jobs to stay internal-only, that's a configuration decision to raise before rollout, not something already built in.
- **Ratings with a cold-start floor.** Three dimensions — punctuality, professionalism, completeness — and a new lawyer's score only becomes visible after 3 completed jobs, so nobody's judged on too little data.
- **WhatsApp-first, with an in-app fallback.** Every notification that goes out on WhatsApp is also logged in-app, so a lawyer with WhatsApp trouble doesn't miss it silently.

---

## What it means for your firm

- **Recaptures billable time.** Less time in transit, more time on higher-value work.
- **No more scrambling.** A signing conflict is a two-minute post instead of a round of individual WhatsApp messages hoping someone's free.
- **Visibility without micromanaging.** You can see who's covering what without being in the loop on every handoff.
- **Nothing changes for your clients.** They still deal with your firm; Law Kaki is purely an internal coordination layer between lawyers.

---

## What to know before you commit

Being upfront about where this stands today:

- **No payment processing.** Commission shown is indicative only — settlement happens outside the platform, however your firm already handles that internally.
- **No Bar Council verification yet.** Registration today is self-serve: a lawyer verifies their phone number via WhatsApp OTP and picks their firm from a list — there's no credential check or admin approval gate in front of that yet. If your firm needs tighter control over who can register under its name before piloting, tell us — that's a scoping conversation, not a rebuild.
- **This is a pilot-stage product**, not a mature SaaS platform. Expect rough edges, and expect the team behind it to want your feedback.

---

## Getting your firm started

1. Lawyers register themselves — a WhatsApp link, a phone number, and a minute to fill in name, firm, and role (post jobs, pick up jobs, or both).
2. No setup required on the firm's end to begin using it.
3. If you want firm-only visibility, or a review step before your lawyers appear on the platform, raise it with the Law Kaki team before rollout — worth deciding deliberately rather than assuming.
